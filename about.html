About E&D Tech Solutions

A team building products that hold up after launch.

We're a technology company rooted in Nairobi, Kenya — focused on helping businesses across East Africa grow through digital products that are built to last, not just to demo well.

01

Who we are

E&D Tech Solutions was founded to bridge the gap between great ideas and great technology. We work with startups, SMEs, and established companies across Kenya to design and build digital products that perform.

Our team brings together web development, mobile app development, system development, AI, design, and IT consulting — all under one roof, right here in Nairobi.

02

What we stand for

We believe Kenyan businesses deserve world-class technology without the world-class price tag — polished, reliable products with strong foundations, built to grow with the business.

Every project is shaped by three things: creative direction, technical depth, and a clear focus on business outcomes.

03

Why work with us

We understand the Kenyan and East African market — from M-Pesa integrations to multilingual interfaces — and build with that context in mind from the first conversation.

We serve clients across Kenya and the wider East Africa region from our Nairobi studio, and we stay reachable long after launch.

Relationships come first. Everything else follows.

We're not trying to be the biggest agency out there — we're trying to be the one you actually want to call when something needs fixing, or when the next idea is ready to build.
